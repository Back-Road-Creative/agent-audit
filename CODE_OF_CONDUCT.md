# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## What is expected

- Give and take feedback on the work itself. "This breaks on empty input" is useful;
  "this is amateurish" is not.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not, and ask before concluding they are
  wrong.
- Accept that maintainers say no to things. A declined pull request is not a verdict on you.
- Keep discussion in the open where it belongs, so decisions have a record.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or bad-faith argument.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and commit messages, and to
anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **joepetjr@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's
identity is not shared.

The maintainer will respond with whatever is warranted — a private word, an edit or removal
of the content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. There is no appeals committee; this
is a small project with one maintainer, and that is the whole process.
